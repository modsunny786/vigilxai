About
VigilAI

VigilAI is a free, browser-based deepfake and AI voice fraud detection platform. It was created to give ordinary people the same fraud-detection capabilities that large organisations have — at zero cost.

As deepfake technology becomes cheaper and accessible to bad actors, so must the defences. VigilAI runs three independent detection layers simultaneously and delivers a verdict in under 5 seconds with no data ever leaving the user's device.

Mission: Make AI-powered voice and face verification instantly accessible to anyone on earth — free, private, and requiring zero technical knowledge to use.
